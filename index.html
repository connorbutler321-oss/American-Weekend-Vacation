Crystal Surfboard Quest

Maya "Rip" Rivers is a Nashville board shaper chasing a family legend: a crystal surfboard hidden at Pensacola Pier. City-themed gangs have blocked the route south, so she rides stormwater channels, city streets, and coast-bound waves to reach the beach before sunset.

Goal: defeat gangs, dodge road hazards, gather shells and repair kits, and claim the crystal surfboard treasure at Pensacola Pier.
